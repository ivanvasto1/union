Frequently Asked Questions

What is a poker union?
A B2B network connecting independent poker clubs into a shared player pool. Clubs in the union share liquidity - more active tables at all hours and stake levels. Unions operate across platforms like PPPoker, X Poker, and ClubGG.
What are the best PPPoker clubs to join in 2026?
The best PPPoker clubs are those operating within large, established unions with verified player traffic and low commission rates. TOP UNION (Club ID 3404) is the highest-ranked option in 2026, offering 170,000+ active players across three platforms at 7% flat commission - the lowest in the market.
How much does it cost to join a poker union?
Most unions charge commission on rake - typically 7-20% depending on the union. No upfront joining fees from reputable operators. TOP UNION charges 7% with no setup cost.
What is the difference between a poker union and a poker club?
A poker club is the individual entity that hosts games and manages players. A poker union is the B2B network that connects multiple clubs into a shared liquidity pool. Club owners join unions to access more players - unions don't interact directly with recreational players.
Do I lose ownership of my club by joining?
No. You retain full ownership of your club, players, and brand. The union provides the liquidity layer - invisible to your players, valuable to your bottom line.

What is a poker agent and how do they relate to unions?
A poker agent recruits players into clubs and earns a share of the rake those players generate. Agents work within the club structure - unions operate at the B2B level above agents and clubs. Some unions offer agent partnership programs; TOP UNION offers a dedicated agent partner program.
