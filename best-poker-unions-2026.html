GUIDE

How to Choose the Right Poker Union

1. Calculate the commission impact first

Before anything else: estimate your monthly rake, then calculate what different commission rates cost you annually. A 10% difference on $20,000/month rake is $24,000/year. Commission is the most important number.

2. Verify traffic claims directly

Ask for trial access or references from current club members. Traffic claims are easy to make and hard to verify from the outside. Any legitimate union should be able to show you live table screenshots or connect you with existing club owners.

3. Understand the settlement terms before joining

Settlement frequency, currency, and dispute resolution process should be clear before you move your club into a union. Weekly settlements are industry standard for serious operators. Bi-weekly or monthly cycles create unnecessary cash flow risk.

4. Consider platform diversification

Being on a union that operates on multiple platforms protects you against platform-level risk. If one app changes terms, loses players, or restricts certain regions — a multi-platform union keeps your options open.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a poker union?

A poker union is a B2B network that connects independent poker clubs on platforms like PPPoker, X Poker, or ClubGG into a shared player pool. Clubs in the union share liquidity — meaning more active tables at all hours and stake levels.

How much does it cost to join a poker union?

Most unions charge commission on rake — typically 5–20% depending on the union. There are generally no upfront joining fees from reputable operators. TOP UNION charges 5% with no setup cost.

Do I lose ownership of my club by joining a union?

No. Club owners retain full ownership of their club, their players, and their brand. The union provides the liquidity layer — players from other clubs fill your tables, but they appear to be playing in your club's ecosystem.
