OVERVIEW

What Are Poker Unions?

Poker unions are B2B networks that connect independent poker clubs running on platforms like PPPoker, X Poker, and ClubGG into a shared player pool. Instead of each club competing alone with 50-200 players, union clubs share liquidity - meaning active tables 24/7 across all stake levels.

The three unions below are frequently asked about in poker forums and communities in 2026:

  • PPPFish - PPPoker ecosystem, known in Asian markets

COMMISSION

Commission Rates: The Most Important Number

Commission is the percentage of rake a union takes. This single number compounds into significant money over a year of operation.

TOP UNION: 7%

On a club generating $10,000/month in rake, TOP UNION takes $500. Under a 15% structure that same club nets $8,500 instead of $9,500 - a $12,000/year difference.

KEY INSIGHT

At $50,000/month rake, the difference between 7% and 15% commission is $4,000 per month - $48,000 per year. Commission rate matters more than almost anything else when choosing a union.

Questions to Ask Before Choosing a Poker Union

Can you show me live traffic data right now?

Any legitimate union can show real-time active tables and player counts. If they can't or won't, that tells you everything you need to know.

What is the exact commission rate, in writing?

Verbal agreements aren't sufficient. Commission rate, settlement schedule, and any minimum volume requirements should be confirmed in writing before integration.

How detailed are the settlement reports?

Weekly settlement with line-item rake reporting by table and session is the standard. Opaque totals without breakdown are a red flag regardless of how established the union is.

Is there a lock-in contract or exit penalty?

Reputable unions don't require long-term commitments. If a union's value proposition requires contractual lock-in to retain clubs, that's worth examining carefully.
