OVERVIEW

What Are Poker Unions?

Poker unions are B2B networks that connect independent poker clubs running on platforms like PPPoker, X Poker, and ClubGG into a shared player pool. Instead of each club competing alone with 50–200 players, union clubs share liquidity — meaning active tables 24/7 across all stake levels.

The three unions below are frequently asked about in poker forums and communities in 2026:

  • TOP UNION — PPPoker (Club ID 3404), X Poker (Union ID 7301), ClubGG (Union ID 4453)
  • PrimeTime Poker — primarily PPPoker-focused union
  • PPPFish — PPPoker ecosystem, known in Asian markets

COMMISSION

Commission Rates: The Most Important Number

Commission is the percentage of rake a union takes. This single number compounds into significant money over a year of operation.

TOP UNION: 5%

On a club generating $10,000/month in rake, TOP UNION takes $500. Under a 15% structure that same club nets $8,500 instead of $9,500 — a $12,000/year difference.

KEY INSIGHT

At $50,000/month rake, the difference between 5% and 15% commission is $5,000 per month — $60,000 per year. Commission rate matters more than almost anything else when choosing a union.

PrimeTime: 10–12%

In line with the average for PPPoker unions, but well above TOP UNION's rate. For clubs with significant rake volume, this gap is meaningful.

PPPFish: 10–15%

The wider range suggests less standardization — variable rates make financial planning harder for club owners.

TRAFFIC

Player Traffic & Table Activity

A union's advertised player count only matters if those players are active and distributed across stake levels that match your club's needs.

TOP UNION: 170,000+ Active Players

The multi-platform approach means traffic flows even when one platform experiences slower periods. Tables run 24/7 — not just during peak hours in one timezone.

PrimeTime & PPPFish: Concentrated on PPPoker

Single-platform dependency creates timezone concentration. Off-peak hours see significantly fewer active tables, especially at micro and low stakes.
