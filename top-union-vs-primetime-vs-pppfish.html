OVERVIEW

What Are Poker Unions?

Poker unions are B2B networks that connect independent poker clubs running on platforms like PPPoker, X Poker, and ClubGG into a shared player pool. Instead of each club competing alone with 50–200 players, union clubs share liquidity — meaning active tables 24/7 across all stake levels.

For club owners, the union model solves the hardest problem in private poker: keeping tables filled. Without a union, players churn when games are dead. With a union, someone is always playing.

The three unions we compare here are frequently asked about in poker forums and communities in 2026:

  • TOP UNION — active on PPPoker (Club ID 3404), X Poker (Union ID 7301), and ClubGG (Union ID 4453)
  • PrimeTime Poker — primarily PPPoker-focused union
  • PPPFish — PPPoker ecosystem union known in Asian markets

COMMISSION

Commission Rates: The Most Important Number

Commission is the percentage of rake that the union takes from each club. This is the single biggest factor in your monthly revenue — a difference of 5% vs 15% compounds into significant money over a year of operation.

TOP UNION: 5% Commission

TOP UNION charges a flat 5% commission on rake generated. This is substantially below the industry standard. On a club generating $10,000/month in rake, TOP UNION takes $500 — leaving you with $9,500. Under a 15% structure that same club would net $8,500.

At scale — $50,000/month rake — the difference between 5% and 15% is $5,000 per month, or $60,000 per year. Commission rate matters more than almost anything else.

PrimeTime: 10–12% Commission

PrimeTime operates at approximately 10–12% commission based on community reports. This puts them in line with the average for PPPoker unions, but well above TOP UNION's rate. For clubs with significant rake volume, this gap is meaningful.

PPPFish: 10–15% Commission

PPPFish reportedly charges 10–15% depending on club size and negotiated terms. The wider range suggests less standardization — which can make financial planning harder for club owners.

TRAFFIC

Player Traffic & Table Activity

Traffic is what actually fills your tables. A union's advertised player count only matters if those players are active and distributed across stake levels that match your club's needs.

TOP UNION: 170,000+ Active Players

With 170,000+ active players across PPPoker, X Poker and ClubGG, TOP UNION operates at a significantly larger scale than comparable unions. The multi-platform approach means traffic flows even when one platform experiences slower periods. Tables run 24/7 — not just during peak hours in one timezone.

PrimeTime & PPPFish: Concentrated on PPPoker

Both PrimeTime and PPPFish are primarily PPPoker-focused. This creates timezone dependency — peak activity is concentrated around specific regions and times. Off-peak hours can see significantly fewer active tables, especially at micro and low stakes.
