FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT IS A POKER NETWORK PARTNER?

A poker network partner is an agent, club owner, or operator who connects their players to a shared liquidity network. Instead of running an isolated club, you get access to active tables 24/7 across 80+ clubs and earn weekly from the volume your players generate.

HOW DO POKER AGENTS EARN MONEY?

Poker agents earn a share of the rake generated by their players. More hands played means more rake, which means higher earnings. Income depends entirely on player activity, stakes, and retention — there are no guaranteed fixed amounts.

DO I NEED MY OWN CLUB TO BECOME A PARTNER?

No. You can join as an agent with an existing player base, as a club owner connecting your existing club, or as an operator building a new network. What matters is that you already have access to poker players.

WHICH PLATFORMS DOES TOP UNION SUPPORT?

TOP UNION operates on PPPoker (Club ID 3404), X Poker (Union ID 7301), and ClubGG (Union ID 4453). You can partner on any or all three platforms.

HOW DO SETTLEMENTS WORK FOR PARTNERS?

Settlements are weekly. You get clear reporting and visibility on your players' activity, with transparent terms and regular payout cycles.
