What Club Owners Should Know Before Joining a Poker Network

Running a poker club on PPPoker, X Poker, or ClubGG is a business - and like any business, profitability depends on volume. The fundamental challenge is that volume requires players, players require active games, and active games require volume. Breaking out of that cycle is what a union network makes possible.

When you join TOP UNION, your club connects to a shared player pool of 170,000+ registered members across 80+ clubs. Your players don't change platforms. They don't change apps. They just see more games when they open their existing app. For you, that translates into more rake, more consistent activity, and less time managing player recruitment.

The Commission Model Explained

TOP UNION charges 7% on rake generated through the union pool by your players. This is the only fee - no monthly subscription, no setup cost, no per-player charge. The model creates alignment: we benefit when your players are active, so our incentive is to keep the network running well.

Settlements happen weekly. You receive a clear breakdown of rake volume, commission, and net payout. There are no hidden fees or adjustments after settlement. If you have questions about how the numbers are calculated, we show you exactly how they work before you commit to joining.
