FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TOP UNION?

TOP UNION is a B2B poker infrastructure provider that connects independent poker clubs into a shared liquidity network. We operate across PPPoker, X Poker and ClubGG - three of the largest private poker platforms in the world. Club owners retain full ownership while gaining access to a 170,000+ player network.

Which platforms does TOP UNION support?

TOP UNION operates on PPPoker (Club ID 3404), X Poker (Union ID 7301), and ClubGG (Union ID 4453). This multi-platform presence is unique among major poker unions in 2026 and gives club owners the flexibility to operate across multiple apps simultaneously.

What commission does TOP UNION charge?

TOP UNION charges a flat 7% commission on rake - significantly below the industry standard of 10-20% charged by most unions. There are no setup fees, no monthly minimums, and no hidden charges. Settlements are weekly with full transparency.

How long does it take to join?

Integration typically takes 24-48 hours from initial contact. No technical work is required from the club owner. Contact us via Telegram or WhatsApp with your Club ID or Union ID and we will handle everything on our side.

Do I lose control of my club by joining TOP UNION?

No. Club owners retain full ownership and control over their club, players, and brand. TOP UNION provides the network infrastructure layer only - invisible to your players, but immediately visible in your table activity and rake volume.

How are settlements handled?

TOP UNION settles weekly with detailed reports showing rake per table and per session. All figures are transparent and auditable. Partners receive payment on a consistent weekly schedule with no delays.

How We Work with Club Owners

TOP UNION operates as a silent infrastructure layer. We don't compete with your club's brand, interfere with your player relationships, or impose operational requirements that change how you run your games. Our role is to provide the network connectivity that makes your games viable - and then stay out of the way.

When a club owner joins our network, the first thing they notice is consistent table activity at hours that used to be dead. That's the most direct measure of what we provide: games running when they previously weren't. Everything else - commission structure, onboarding, support - is built around making that outcome reliable.

We work across three platforms - PPPoker, X Poker, and ClubGG - because different club owners have different preferences, and because diversification across platforms creates a more stable overall network. If you're already running on one platform and want to expand to another, we can support that transition.
