ABOUT TOP UNION

The Global Poker Club Network

TOP UNION is a B2B poker infrastructure provider connecting independent poker clubs into a shared liquidity network across PPPoker, X Poker and ClubGG.

What We Do

We connect independent poker clubs running on PPPoker, X Poker and ClubGG into a unified player pool. Instead of competing with isolated bases of 50-100 players, club owners tap into a shared network of 170,000+ players across 80+ clubs.

The result: tables stay active 24/7 at all stakes. Players stop leaving for larger networks. Rake grows without recruiting new players from scratch.

Club owners retain full ownership of their club, players and brand. We provide the network layer and liquidity — nothing changes on their side.
