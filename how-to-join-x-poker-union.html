X Poker is one of the fastest-growing private poker platforms of 2025–2026. If you own or manage a club on X Poker, joining the right union is the single most effective way to grow your player base and keep tables active around the clock.

BASICS

What Is an X Poker Union?

X Poker is a mobile-first private poker platform where clubs operate as independent entities — you control the rules, stakes, and access.

A union connects multiple clubs together into a shared player pool. When your club joins a union, players from all clubs in that network appear at your tables — and your players can join tables across the union. Instead of waiting for your 50 members to be online simultaneously, you're drawing from thousands of active players.

WHY JOIN

Why Join an X Poker Union?

More active tables, 24/7

The biggest problem for any private club is dead tables — times when no one is playing. A union solves this by spreading activity across many clubs and time zones. Someone is always active.

Player retention

Players leave when games aren't running. With union traffic feeding your tables, players stay because there's always a game. Retention directly impacts your rake long-term.

All stake levels covered

Small clubs often can't sustain games across all stakes. A large union has enough volume to keep micro, low, mid, and high stakes active simultaneously — serving recreational players and serious grinders in the same club.

No change to your ownership

Joining a union doesn't mean giving up your club. You retain full ownership, control over players, and your club identity. The union is infrastructure — invisible to players, valuable to your bottom line.

STEP BY STEP

How to Join an X Poker Union

01
Find Your X Poker Club ID

Your Club ID is the numeric identifier for your club in X Poker — visible in your club management dashboard inside the app. Have it ready before reaching out to any union.

If you're a player (not a club owner) looking to join via an agent, you'll need your Player ID instead.

02
Research Unions — Ask the Right Questions

Before committing, ask any union: How many active players do you have? What's your commission rate? How does settlement work? Can I speak to an existing club owner?

Any legitimate union should answer directly. Vague answers or pressure to commit without data are red flags.

03
Contact the Union & Get a Traffic Preview

Reach out via the union's official channel. For TOP UNION: Telegram or WhatsApp. Share your Club ID and current situation.

A good union gives you a realistic preview of expected traffic — not just a sales pitch. TOP UNION shows active table data before any commitment.

04
Review Terms & Commission

Confirm: the commission percentage, settlement frequency, how disputes are handled, exit terms.

WATCH OUT

Commission rates vary wildly. TOP UNION charges 7%. Many unions charge 10–20%. On $15K/month rake, the difference adds up to thousands per year.

05
Integration & Go Live

The technical integration is handled entirely by the union — no technical work required from you. For TOP UNION: 24–48 hours from contact to live tables.

Your existing players are not disrupted. Union players simply start appearing at your tables as the network connection goes live.
