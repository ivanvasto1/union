BASICS

What Is an X Poker Union?

X Poker is a mobile-first private poker platform that lets individuals and groups create clubs and run their own games. Each club operates as an independent entity — you control the rules, stakes, and access.

A union on X Poker is a network layer that connects multiple clubs together into a shared player pool. When your club joins a union, players from all clubs in that union appear at your tables — and your players can join tables in other clubs in the network.

The result: instead of waiting for your 50 members to be online at the same time, you're drawing from a pool of thousands of active players across the union. Tables fill. Games run. Revenue grows.

WHY JOIN

Why Join an X Poker Union?

More active tables, 24/7

The biggest problem for any private poker club is dead tables — times of day when no one is playing. A union solves this by spreading activity across many clubs and time zones. Someone is always active.

Player retention

Players leave clubs when games aren't running. With union traffic feeding your tables, players stay because there's always a game to join. Retention directly impacts your rake over time.

All stake levels covered

Small clubs often can't sustain games across all stake levels — micro, low, mid, and high. A large union has enough volume to keep all levels active simultaneously. This lets you serve recreational players and serious grinders in the same club.

No change to your ownership

Joining a union doesn't mean giving up your club. You retain full ownership, control over your players, and your club identity. The union is infrastructure — invisible to your players, valuable to your bottom line.

STEP BY STEP

How to Join an X Poker Union: 5 Steps

01
Find Your X Poker Club ID

Your Club ID is the numeric identifier for your club in X Poker. You'll find it in your club management dashboard inside the app. It's typically a 4–6 digit number. Have this ready before reaching out to any union.

If you're a player (not a club owner) looking to join a union through an agent, you'll need your Player ID instead.

02
Research Unions — Ask the Right Questions

Not all X Poker unions are equal. Before committing, ask any union you're considering: How many active players do you have? What's your commission rate? How does settlement work? Can I speak to an existing club owner in your network?

Any legitimate union should be able to answer these directly. Vague answers or pressure to commit without data are red flags.

03
Contact the Union & Get a Traffic Preview

Reach out via the union's official contact channel — for TOP UNION, that's Telegram or WhatsApp. Share your Club ID and current traffic situation.

A good union will give you a realistic preview of what traffic to expect — not just a sales pitch. TOP UNION shows prospective clubs active table data before any commitment.

04
Review Terms & Commission

Before integration, make sure you understand: the commission percentage on rake, the settlement frequency and process, how disputes are handled, and whether there are any exit terms if you want to leave the union later.

Commission rates vary significantly. TOP UNION charges 5%. Many unions charge 10–20%. On $15,000/month in rake, a 10% difference is $18,000 per year.

05
Integration & Go Live

The technical integration is handled by the union — you don't need to do any technical work. For TOP UNION, the process typically takes 24–48 hours from initial contact to live tables.

Your existing players are not disrupted. The union players simply start appearing at your tables as the network connection goes live.
