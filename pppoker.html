What Is a PPPoker Union?

A PPPoker union, also called a PPPoker alliance, is a B2B network that connects independent PPPoker clubs into a shared player pool. PPPoker Club ID 3404 - TOP UNION's club identifier - gives partner clubs direct access to one of the largest active liquidity networks on the PPPoker platform.

PPPoker operates on a private club model: each club owner recruits their own players and manages their own community. The limitation is that isolated clubs can only run games when their own members are online. A PPPoker union solves this by connecting multiple clubs into one shared player pool - so tables fill regardless of which club's members are logged in.

When you join the TOP UNION PPPoker network, your club gains access to 170,000+ active players across 80+ partner clubs. Your members stop waiting for games and start playing - at any stake level, at any hour.

PPPoker was built around a club-and-union structure that gives independent club owners real control over their games. That's the central challenge for most PPPoker club owners: getting enough active players across enough time zones to keep games running consistently.
